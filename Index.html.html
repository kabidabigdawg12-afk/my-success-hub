90-day roadmap

Days 1–14 · Set the base

Build a manageable routine, prepare for school, and try short sessions across your interests.

Days 15–30 · Explore

Try beginner finance lessons, follow a reliable world-news source, explore biomedical engineering, and ask what club students need.

Days 31–60 · Build

Choose one small project: a club proposal, a finance glossary, a science explainer, or a service-hours tracker.

Days 61–90 · Review and refine

Review what you enjoyed, what helped, and what realistically fits your school life. Pick the next step.

Useful research habits

  • Write down the source and date.
  • Compare two trustworthy sources for major claims.
  • Separate facts from opinions and predictions.
  • Save questions you want to return to rather than researching endlessly.
